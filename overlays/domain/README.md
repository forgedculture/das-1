# Domain overlays

Domain overlays tighten DAS-1(TM) for high-impact operating contexts.

Rule
- Domain overlays may tighten core controls and add catalog controls.
- Domain overlays must not weaken core controls.
- Domain overlays do not replace legal, regulatory, policy, or mission-specific compliance review.

Available examples
- `overlays/domain/business/`: enterprise operations, HR, procurement, customer operations, support, and internal workflows.
- `overlays/domain/healthcare/`: healthcare administration, PHI boundaries, patient communications, chart operations, claims, and clinical-accountability boundaries.
- `overlays/domain/law/`: legal research, drafting, matter management, e-discovery, privilege, client communications, and filings.
- `overlays/domain/finance/`: banking, payments, trading support, ledgers, lending, insurance, treasury, compliance operations, and financial advice workflows.
- `overlays/domain/voting-elections/`: election administration support, voter registration workflows, ballot logistics, public information, reporting support, audits, and election operations.
- `overlays/domain/government/`: civilian public services, benefits, permits, procurement, records, public communications, enforcement support, and administrative decision systems.
- `overlays/domain/military/`: defense administration, logistics, readiness, cyber defense support, intelligence-support workflows, mission planning support, and command-support systems.
