# Trademarks (DAS-1(TM))

This project is open. The branding is not automatically open.

## What is a trademark here
- "Delegated Authority Standard(TM)"
- "DAS-1(TM)"
- "DAS-1 Conformant(TM)"
- Project logos, badges, and word marks (collectively, the "Marks")

All Marks are owned by the project owner. The open licenses for the spec and any code do not grant trademark rights.

## Allowed (no permission needed)
- Referencing the project by name in articles, talks, and documentation.
- Linking to the canonical spec and repository.
- Stating factual compatibility in plain language, without using the Marks as product branding.

## Conformance claim policy
You may use the phrase:
- "DAS-1(TM) v0.002 Conformant"

Only if you meet the conformance criteria in:
- spec/conformance/conformance-criteria.md

If you make a conformance claim, you MUST disclose:
- The DAS-1(TM) version
- Date of last drills (D1 and D2)
- Known exceptions with expiry
- A non-certification statement (self-asserted claim, not third-party certification)

You MUST NOT:
- Claim or imply certification, endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation unless you have written permission.
- Use "DAS-1 Conformant(TM)" as your product name.

## Logo and badge usage
- The project logo and any "Conformant" badge artwork may only be used in compliance with spec/conformance/badge-usage.md.
- If you are unsure, do not use the logo.

## Enforcement
If you misuse the Marks, the project owner may request corrections, removal, or updates to your claims.


## How to use (TM) and (R)

- Use (TM) for unregistered marks.
- Use (R) only after the mark is federally registered in the relevant jurisdiction.
- Do not use (R) until you have an active registration.
